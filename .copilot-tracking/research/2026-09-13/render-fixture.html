Equations and meaning

The prediction is y^=wx+b\hat{y}=wx+by^​=wx+b. A fraction 12\frac{1}{2}21​ means one half. Parenthesis-delimited math also works: x∈Rdx \in \mathbb{R}^{d}x∈Rd.

L(w)=1N∑i=1N(wxi−yi)2L(w)=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left(wx_i-y_i\right)^2L(w)=N1​i=1∑N​(wxi​−yi​)2

This loss is the mean squared difference between the prediction and target. N is the number of examples, x is the input, y is the target, and w is a weight.

∂L∂w=2N∑i=1N(wxi−yi)xi\frac{\partial L}{\partial w}=\frac{2}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}(wx_i-y_i)x_i∂w∂L​=N2​i=1∑N​(wxi​−yi​)xi​

The partial derivative measures how the loss changes when the weight changes.

ddxx2=2xW∈Rdout×din\frac{d}{dx}x^2=2x \qquad W\in\mathbb{R}^{d_{out}\times d_{in}}dxd​x2=2xW∈Rdout​×din​

The derivative of x squared is twice x. The matrix W has output-dimension rows and input-dimension columns. These sentences preserve the meaning if copying individual mathematical glyphs from a PDF loses their two-dimensional order.
